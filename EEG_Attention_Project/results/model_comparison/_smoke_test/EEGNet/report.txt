EEGNet Model Comparison: independent from B0/E1/E2/E3.
summary.csv: per-group five-fold means and pooled metrics.
model_comparison.csv: paired fold-average difference vs pre-existing E1 1D-CNN.
comparison.png: E1 1D-CNN vs EEGNet, when E1 reference exists.
eegnet_channels.png: EEGNet-only channel comparison with numeric labels.
All metrics refer to RT percentile proxy labels, not ground-truth attention.
